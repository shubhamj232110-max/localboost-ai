FAQs

Questions about LocalBoost AI

Answers to common questions about the service and plan request.

Is the content completely ready to post?

You may receive captions, Reel scripts and content ideas that you can adapt. Before posting, check your business name, offer, prices, dates and contact details.

Can I get Hindi or Hinglish captions?

Yes. Select Hindi, Hinglish or English in the plan request form. The requested language and your business details help guide the content.

How does payment work?

This website does not currently collect online payments. Submitting a plan request does not charge you. Any paid offer, amount and payment method will be confirmed clearly beforehand.

Will a plan be generated as soon as I submit?

No. This demo does not automatically generate a plan. Submitted requests are reviewed, and the confirmation page confirms receipt.

How do I request a plan?

Click “Get my plan”, complete the form steps, and submit your business details, marketing goal and contact email.
