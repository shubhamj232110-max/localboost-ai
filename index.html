FAQs

LocalBoost AI ke baare mein sawal

Hamari service, content aur plan request ke baare mein aam sawalon ke jawab.

Kya content bilkul ready-to-post hota hai?

Aapko copy-paste ke liye tayyar captions, Reel scripts aur content ideas mil sakte hain. Post karne se pehle apne business ka naam, offer, price, dates aur contact details zaroor check karein.

Kya Hindi ya Hinglish captions milenge?

Haan. Plan request form mein aap Hindi, Hinglish ya English select kar sakte hain. Content aapki local audience aur business ke hisaab se tayyar kiya jayega.

Payment kaise karni hogi?

Abhi website online payment collect nahi karti. Plan request bhejne se payment nahi hoti. Agar kisi paid service ka offer available hua, toh payment method aur amount pehle clearly confirm kiye jayenge.

Kya form submit karte hi AI plan mil jayega?

Nahi. Abhi request submit hone ke baad details review ki jaati hain; plan automatically generate nahi hota. Form submit karne par aapko confirmation page dikhai dega.

Plan request kaise bhejun?

“Free plan banayein” button par click karke form ke steps poore karein. Business details, marketing goal aur contact email bharne ke baad request submit karein.
