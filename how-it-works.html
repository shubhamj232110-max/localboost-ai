Marketing plan banana ab confusing nahi hoga.

LocalBoost AI aapke business details ko practical marketing actions mein convert karta hai.

3 simple steps

Kaise kaam karta hai?

Start karne ke liye technical ya marketing experience ki zarurat nahi hai.

01

Business ke baare mein batayein

Business name, category, city, audience, main offer aur marketing goal select karein.

02

AI aapka plan banata hai

AI aapke details ke basis par captions, Reel scripts, content calendar aur offer ideas prepare karta hai.

03

Edit, post aur grow karein

Plan ko review karein, apne brand ke hisaab se edit karein aur Instagram/WhatsApp par use karein.

Har AI-generated offer, price, claim aur message ko publish karne se pehle business owner ko verify karna chahiye.
